Four filthy walls · one roller

FreshCoat

A room caked in soot, mould and old spray paint. One roller, and it keeps running dry. Roll straight through the corners, top up, and don't stop until every wall is a colour you chose.

03 — Controls

Everything is one hand

Two of these aren't guessable, so they're worth reading before you start.

WASD Move the roller. It's always against the wall, so moving is painting.
Space Hold to refill — anywhere in the room. There's no bucket to walk back to.
Shift Slow the roller down for edges and corners.
1–8 Load a colour. Takes effect on the next stroke.
R Strip it back and start the room again.
→→ Keep going past a wall's edge and you round the corner onto the next one.
